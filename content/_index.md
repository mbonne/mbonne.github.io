title: "Build Test Run"
---

Build Test Run covers homelabbing, infrastructure, monitoring, automation, scripting, and AI tooling: the decisions, trade-offs, and undocumented behaviour that only show up once you have skin in the game.

**Topics:** containerisation and compose stacks, Prometheus/Grafana/Loki/Vector monitoring, shell scripting and automation, local LLM infrastructure, and hardening public-facing services (Cloudflare, Traefik, Authelia).

Written for people running their own servers, past the basics. No publishing schedule, no padding, no dumbed-down explainers.
